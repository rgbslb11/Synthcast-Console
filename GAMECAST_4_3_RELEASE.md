# GameCast 4.3 — Week 4 operating slate

Status: RELEASE CANDIDATE / STAGING. Chairman authorized a new 4.3 issue for Week 4 using the 4.2.3 UI and existing football-engine behavior. This release does not merge production, publish SEUD, accept results, or modify prior releases.

## Authorized scope

1. Create a new `release/gamecast-v4.3` branch from the verified 4.2.3 branch.
2. Move the operating slate to canonical Week 4 (`2026-W04`) with 55 games.
3. Preserve the 4.2.3 Chairman/UI interaction hardening.
4. Preserve the existing 4.2.2 football/play/timing mechanics without retuning.
5. Isolate the backend as `gamecast-week4-v4-3`, engine identity `GC-W4-V4.3-RC1`, and session namespace `w4v43-`.
6. Do not silently promote the post-Week-3 shadow FBS TEAM/OFF/DEF candidate. Carry the prior sidecar forward unchanged until a separate ratings authorization/load.

## Schedule and TV evidence

Schedule authority is `2026_FBS_Schedule_LOCKED_v5.xlsx`: Week 4 contains 55 unique games, G0115 through G0169. Neutral-site flags are G0119 Kansas at Arizona State and G0164 West Virginia at Virginia.

TV/carriage authority is `2026_TV_W04_IMMUTABLE_MASTER_v1_QUARANTINE.xlsx`. It validates 55 source rows but only 54 exact ledger joins. `W4-BLOCK-001` remains unresolved: source row W4-CAR-018 says Colorado at Nebraska while locked schedule G0142 says Colorado at Northwestern. 4.3 therefore keeps G0142 as Colorado at Northwestern and intentionally sets kickoff/network to `TBD` / `TV TBD`; it does not patch the quarantined TV master.

## Ratings boundary

The backend carries forward the prior 4.2.2 `power.ts` byte-for-byte. No Week 4 FBS rating candidate is promoted by this release. At build-time schedule/power coverage from that carry-forward is 32 of 55 games power-ready and 23 power-pending. AUTO remains blocked on power-pending games by the existing engine rule.

The Chairman separately established the doctrine that an FCS opponent is rated at 10% of the FBS opponent it faces. This release does not manufacture FCS values from unapproved Week 4 FBS shadow grades. The rule remains for the subsequent authorized Week 4 power load.

## Mechanics preservation

The 4.3 backend is generated deterministically by copying `gamecast-week3-v4-2-2` and replacing only weekly schedule/identity/governance bindings. Football functions including RNG, edge, strategy, play generation, timing, TV-break handling, Delay, Edit, continuation seed, acceleration, and result lifecycle are not retuned.

The 4.3 UI is generated by copying `public/v4.2.3`; only API binding, local-storage namespace, visible Week 4/release copy, and release identity are changed. Edit/focus preservation and background-sync semantics remain the 4.2.3 implementation.
